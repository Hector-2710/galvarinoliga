# Galvarino Liga Fútbol

The official site of the amateur football league of **Galvarino**, a commune in Chile’s Araucanía Region.

This is where the community follows the championship: fixtures, results, standings, clubs, and goal scorers — updated as the season unfolds.

## Follow the season

- **Home** — Upcoming matches and the current table
- **Fixture** — The full match calendar, matchday by matchday, with scores and status
- **Match pages** — Scoreline, goal scorers, date, pitch, and matchday for every game
- **Clubs** — Each participating team: founding year, board, history, and top scorers
- **Standings** — Points, matches played, goals for and against, and goal difference
- **Statistics** — Season goal-scoring ranking
- **Donations** — A way to support the league and its digital platform

Squad lists and coaching staff will be added later.

## Support the league

Galvarino Liga Fútbol exists to give communal football a clear, public home online — so families, players, and fans can follow every date.

If you would like to help keep the platform running and growing (including future series such as women’s football and age-group competitions), visit the donations page on the site.

Local businesses can also become **sponsors**. Weekly, hundreds of people check fixtures, results, and stats. Sponsorship puts your brand in front of players, club officials, fans, and families, and shows support for sport in Galvarino.
